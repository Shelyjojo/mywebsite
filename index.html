About Me

A little more about who I am

I'm Joan, and I've always believed that black is not just a color — it's a feeling. It's the quiet confidence of a midnight sky, the depth of a shadow, the elegance that doesn't need to shout to be heard.

I find beauty in minimalism and power in simplicity. Whether it's the clothes I wear, the art I admire, or the spaces I create, I'm drawn to the bold and the understated.

When I'm not lost in thought, you'll find me exploring photography, reading classic literature, or collecting moments that feel like they belong in a black-and-white film.
